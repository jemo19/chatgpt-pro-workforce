# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Respect privacy, permissions, security boundaries, and informed consent.
- Keep examples free of credentials, private prompts, customer data, and
  unrelated personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and project direction.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private
information, unsafe testing, credential solicitation, or repeated disruption is
not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, reject, lock, or remove contributions and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer through their
GitHub profile without publishing sensitive details. If the concern involves a
security or privacy exposure, use the repository's private vulnerability
reporting channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project.
